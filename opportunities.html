Opportunities & Enrichment

Clinical programs, research, and experiences you can pursue while at a California community college.

Clinical & Pre-Health Opportunities

Use these options to build hands-on clinical experience and certifications while you are completing your transfer coursework.

  • Eugene and Ruth Roberts Summer Student Academy — City of Hope
    Summer research experience in cancer and biomedical sciences at City of Hope.
  • COPE Health Scholars
    Structured clinical volunteer program that lets you work alongside nurses and other providers.
  • EMT Certification
    Emergency Medical Technician training that can lead to entry-level clinical jobs and experience.
  • CNA Certification
    Certified Nursing Assistant program that can help you get bedside experience while in school.
  • Medical Assistant (MA) Certification
    Training for work as a medical assistant in clinics or physician offices.
  • Phlebotomy Certification
    Program focused on blood draws and basic clinical lab assistant skills.
  • CPR / BLS Certification
    Basic Life Support (BLS) / CPR training that many clinical roles require.
  • Shadowing local doctors or RNs
    Ask clinics, hospitals, or providers you know if they allow student observers; start with your own doctor or local community clinics.
  • Join clubs!
    Look for pre-med, pre-health, or service clubs on campus to find peers and additional opportunities.

Biomedical Engineering & Research

Programs and ideas for students interested in biomedical engineering, medical devices, or research.

  • CHOC Mi4 Summer Internship
    Innovation and research experience at CHOC (Children's Hospital of Orange County).
  • University Lab Partners (ULP)
    Wet-lab and medtech coworking space; look for internships, events, or programs for community college students.
  • IVC Research Symposium
    Present or explore research projects led by community college students and faculty.
  • Cold email research
    Reach out directly to professors or labs whose work interests you; briefly share your background and why you want to help.
  • Eugene and Ruth Roberts Summer Student Academy — City of Hope
    Also a strong option if you are interested in biomedical engineering or translational research.
  • Join clubs!
    Look for engineering clubs, robotics teams, or design competitions at your college.

Computer Science & Engineering

Search these program names online to find current deadlines and eligibility; many welcome or explicitly support community college students.

  • CalTech SURF — Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowships at Caltech.
  • Project RAISE at CSUF — Research and support for STEM students at CSU Fullerton.
  • Stanford SURF — Summer research program for aspiring researchers at Stanford.
  • Stanford SURGE — Summer Undergraduate Research in Geoscience and Engineering.
  • UCI PRIME Program — Community-focused medical education track at UC Irvine; explore outreach and pipeline opportunities as a CC student.
  • NASA Community College Aerospace Scholars (NCAS) — Online and in-person NASA experience for community college students.
  • NASA Lucy Mission Internship — Opportunities related to NASA's Lucy mission.
  • NASA L'SPACE — Online academy that simulates NASA mission design and teamwork.
  • Join clubs! — CS, engineering, robotics, and hackathon clubs are great ways to build projects and find teammates.
