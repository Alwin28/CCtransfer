Talk to a Counselor

For personalized guidance on your transfer plan, course selection, and application strategy, speak with a counselor at your community college. They can help you:

  • Create or review your Student Education Plan (SEP)
  • Verify your major preparation and GE requirements
  • Navigate TAG, IGETC, and transfer deadlines
  • Get advice tailored to your specific situation

Find your college's counseling office through your student portal or campus website.

Official Resources

For the most up-to-date and official information, always check these sources:

  • UC Admissions — Requirements, deadlines, and campus info for University of California
  • CSU Transfer — California State University transfer requirements and application
  • California Community Colleges — Official CCC Chancellor's Office website
  • ASSIST.org — Course articulation agreements between CC and UC/CSU

Disclaimer: This website is created by students, for students. We are not affiliated with the California Community Colleges, UC, or CSU systems. Information may change — always verify with official sources and your college counselor.
