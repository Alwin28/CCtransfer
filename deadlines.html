Deadlines and Timelines

Key dates for UC transfer applications and major private university transfer deadlines for Fall 2026 entry.

UC Transfer Deadlines — Fall 2026 Admission

These dates summarize the typical University of California (UC) system deadlines for California community college transfers applying for Fall 2026 entry. Always confirm on the official UC Admissions site and individual campus pages, since dates and policies can change.

Application and Submission Deadlines

  • August 1, 2025 — UC application opens for starting and editing.
  • September 1–30, 2025 — UC TAG application filing period (if you are TAG-eligible).
  • October 1 – December 1, 2025 — Fall 2026 UC application filing period. You must submit your UC application by December 1, 2025.
  • July 1–31, 2025 — Winter/Spring 2026 transfer application period (only for campuses/terms that accept mid-year transfers).

Note: UC Santa Cruz has an extended Fall 2026 deadline of December 1 (instead of the usual November 30) for this cycle.

Application Updates and Academic Records

  • December 15, 2025 – January 31, 2026 — Transfer Academic Update (TAU) period. You report your fall grades and planned spring courses. Priority TAU deadline is January 31, 2026.
  • Campuses may allow limited TAU updates beyond this window for additional spring schedule changes.

Financial Aid and Cal Grant

  • October 1, 2025 – March 2, 2026 — FAFSA or California Dream Act Application filing window, plus Cal Grant GPA Verification. Priority deadline is March 2, 2026.

Decisions and Commitment Deadlines

  • March 1, 2026 — UC campuses begin releasing Fall 2026 admission decisions (via campus portals and email).
  • March – May 2026 — Most campuses continue releasing transfer decisions; many finish by April or May.
  • June 1, 2026 — Deadline for admitted transfer students to submit the Statement of Intent to Register (SIR).

Final Documents After Admission

  • July 1, 2026 — Final official transcripts must be postmarked or submitted electronically to your UC campus.
  • July 15, 2026 — Deadline to send official AP/IB test scores or certification documents (for example, IGETC or Cal-GETC certification) if required by your campus.

Quick Summary (Fall 2026)

  • Apply to UC: October 1 – December 1, 2025.
  • TAG filing: September 1–30, 2025.
  • TAU priority deadline: January 31, 2026.
  • FAFSA / California Dream Act / Cal Grant: by March 2, 2026.
  • Decisions begin: March 1, 2026.
  • SIR deadline: June 1, 2026.
  • Final transcripts and scores: July 1–15, 2026.
